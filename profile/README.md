# 👋 Welcome to Core Dumped

**Core Dumped** is the student association of ETSISI (Universidad Politécnica de Madrid) for software engineering, computer engineering, and data science & AI.

We help students get ready for real professional environments through **projects** and **workshops**, connect people who share the same interests, and bring technology to everyone through talks and academies.

## 🚀 Our projects

All our projects live in one place: the **[Project Catalog](https://github.com/orgs/CoreDumped-ETSISI/projects/11)**. There you can see what's active, which projects are looking for members, and which are open to collaboration.

- 🙋 **Want to join a project?** Check the *Join a project* view of the catalog.
- 🤝 **Want to help without joining?** Check the *Collaborate* view. No permission needed.
- 💡 **Have an idea?** [Propose a project](https://github.com/CoreDumped-ETSISI/project-catalog/issues/new?template=project-proposal.yml).

Everything is explained in the [project-catalog](https://github.com/CoreDumped-ETSISI/project-catalog) repository.
